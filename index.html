Abstract

    The Sunshine Skyway Bridge, spanning Tampa Bay, Florida, is a critical infrastructure landmark with a tragic history. 
    In 1980, the original steel cantilever bridge collapsed after a freighter collided with a support pier, killing 35 people.
    This tragedy led to the bridge’s reconstruction as the world’s longest cable-stayed concrete bridge. The new design
    incorporated advanced safety features, such as the Physical Oceanographic Real-Time System (PORTS) for weather and water
    condition monitoring, better bridge flexibility to absorb impacts, and reinforced structural elements like rock islands and 
    dolphins to protect against ship collisions.
